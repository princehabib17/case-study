Overview

Renesource had strong expertise, a solid reputation, and renewable energy experience across the Philippines, but their outdated website hid that value behind walls of text. The redesign focused on visibility, clarity, and lead generation.

Challenge

Renesource already had some of the biggest renewable energy projects in the nation and powerful expertise to back it up. But that value was buried behind dense text and an outdated web experience.

  • Information was hard to scan and trust
  • Project value and impact weren't visible early
  • Lead paths were unclear and fragmented

Our Process

01
Discovery

Notes, sketches, solution mapping. Captured priorities live with the client to define the scope.

02
Research

Competitor audit, target client interviews, persona building. Every UX call traces back here.

03
Prototype Design

Dense content translated into visual sections. Animated globe + impact numbers anchor the home.

04
Asset Requests

Project photos, team info, drone footage, impact numbers. Integrated as they came in.

05
Content, SEO & Development

Webflow build, SEO copy, calculator and icons. Custom dev directed end-to-end.

06
Launch

Animated globe, real footage, lead magnet, MyGo Solar — shipped, indexed, and converting.

Results

The redesign made Renesource's expertise easier to understand, easier to trust, and easier to act on. Projects, impact, and credibility became visible early in the experience.

  • Project showcases became clearer
  • Impact was surfaced visually
  • Lead paths were easier to follow
  • The site felt more credible and future-proof

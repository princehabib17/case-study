Overview

Challenge

Renesource already had some of the biggest renewable energy projects in the nation and powerful expertise to back it up. But that value was buried behind dense text and an outdated web experience.

  • Information was hard to scan and trust
  • Project value and impact weren't visible early
  • Lead paths were unclear and fragmented
The case study

Process is the proof.

Renesource did not need a louder sales page. The work was about uncovering what was already strong, then turning buried expertise into a modern website that could compete on presentation.

A household name with proof buried under an old website.

Renesource had a lot of good projects and strong recognition in the Philippines, but the website looked too old for the level of work behind the brand. During discovery, the core issue became clear: the company had credibility, but the digital experience was hiding it.

  • Strong projects were not being used as immediate proof.
  • The site made a known company feel less current than it was.
  • The first sketch focused on homepage proof, lead capture, credibility, and a solar calculator idea.
  • The sketches were approved before deeper research started.

The competitor sites exposed the market gap.

After the sketches were approved, the research phase focused on competitor websites, market gaps, Renesource's own profile, and the target audience. The audit showed that the space had room for a cleaner, more credible, more modern digital presence.
